Field Update

Building Climate Resilience with Women Small-Scale Farmers in Mampong

Resilience for Africa's Future (RAFO) · Mampong, Ghana · October 2025

In October 2025, Resilience for Africa's Future (RAFO) engaged approximately 30 women small-scale farmers in Mampong, Ghana, through a community training focused on climate adaptation strategies for small-scale farming.

The engagement formed part of RAFO's commitment to working with farming communities around practical responses to climate-related challenges.

During the training, participants explored climate adaptation strategies relevant to small-scale agriculture. Training materials were also provided to support the learning process.

For RAFO, community engagement is an important part of understanding how broader conversations about climate resilience connect with the realities experienced by farmers.

The activity also reflects RAFO's commitment to women's participation in resilient food systems and community-led responses to climate change.

As RAFO continues to develop its work, lessons from direct engagement with farmers and communities will help inform how the organisation approaches sustainable agriculture, food security and climate resilience.

Looking Ahead

RAFO is building its work from the ground up. We aim to continue learning with communities and developing practical approaches that connect sustainable agriculture, food security and climate resilience.
