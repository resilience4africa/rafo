BRIDGE-IT Initiative: Agricultural Technology & Digital Skills Training

Location MOFA-KMA Conference Room, Kumasi, Ghana
Date August 2025
Participants Approximately 100 participants, including agricultural graduates and extension officers
Collaboration In partnership with MOFA-KMA

The BRIDGE-IT Initiative marked the beginning of RAFO's practical work, bringing agricultural graduates and extension officers together for hands-on learning focused on technology and digital tools for agriculture.

Training covered IoT and smart irrigation using Arduino, agricultural data with Power BI, climate adaptation, CV and LinkedIn development, and Google Sites. Practical sessions gave participants an opportunity to engage directly with digital and electronic tools relevant to agriculture.

MOFA-KMA supported the training through the venue, participant mobilisation and logistics. The activity reflects RAFO's commitment to connecting young people and agricultural professionals with practical skills, innovation and opportunities across the agri-food system.

Community Climate Adaptation Training

Location Mampong, Ghana
Date October 2025
Participants Approximately 30 women small-scale farmers
Focus Climate adaptation strategies for small-scale farmers

In October 2025, RAFO engaged approximately 30 women small-scale farmers in Mampong through a community training focused on climate adaptation strategies for small-scale farming.

The engagement provided an opportunity to strengthen participants' understanding of practical approaches to adapting to changing climatic conditions. Training materials were provided to support the learning process.

The activity reflects RAFO's commitment to working directly with farming communities, strengthening women's participation and promoting locally relevant approaches to climate resilience.

In Development

Initiatives in Development

These initiatives are at different stages of development and should not be interpreted as completed programmes.

Agroforestry & Climate-Resilient Tree Growing

RAFO is exploring approaches that bring trees and food production together to strengthen farms, landscapes and community resilience.

The developing initiative is intended to consider areas such as agroforestry, climate-resilient planting materials, tree integration within farming systems, environmental stewardship and landscape restoration.

RAFO is also exploring approaches to improving access to climate-resilient planting materials, including drought-resilient options.

Our longer-term aim is to support approaches in which productive agriculture and healthy ecosystems can reinforce one another.

Household & Backyard Food Growing

RAFO is developing an approach that encourages households with suitable available space to consider small-scale food growing as one contribution to household food security and resilience.

The idea is rooted in a simple principle: food-system resilience can also begin close to home.

RAFO is interested in exploring practical models that can help households make productive use of available space while strengthening awareness of nutrition, food production and environmental responsibility.

Looking Ahead

Our Ambition

RAFO's long-term vision extends beyond individual activities. We want to contribute to an Africa where resilient food systems create opportunities for young people, farming communities are better equipped to respond to climate change, and productive landscapes support both people and ecosystems.

Beginning in Ghana, we aim to learn from practical experience, build meaningful partnerships and develop approaches that can contribute to sustainable agri-food systems and climate-resilient communities over time.

Our ambition is not simply to deliver projects, but to help build systems and communities capable of adapting, learning and thriving.
