Who We Are

A Youth-Led Organisation for Resilient Food Systems

Resilience for Africa's Future (RAFO) is a youth-led and youth-focused organisation based in Accra, Ghana, working to advance sustainable agri-food systems, agroforestry, sustainable agriculture and climate action.

RAFO believes Africa's young people have a critical role to play in building resilient food systems and communities. By equipping and mobilising young people to work alongside farmers and communities, RAFO seeks to promote practical solutions that strengthen food security, create opportunities, restore landscapes and build resilience to climate change.

Our work begins in Ghana, with a vision to contribute to a more resilient and sustainable future across Africa.

Why RAFO Exists

Established in 2025

Resilience for Africa's Future was established in 2025 from a conviction that Africa's young people must be at the centre of building the continent's future.

Across Africa, food insecurity, climate change, environmental degradation and limited economic opportunities are deeply interconnected. At the same time, the continent has a growing generation of young people with the creativity, energy and potential to develop solutions.

RAFO exists to help connect that potential with the needs of farmers, communities and food systems.

Our work brings together sustainable agriculture, agroforestry, technology, skills development and climate action. We seek to create opportunities for young people to learn, innovate and work alongside communities in developing practical solutions that strengthen livelihoods, food security and environmental resilience.

Our Core Belief

Young people are not simply beneficiaries of development. They are partners and drivers of Africa's future.

RAFO places young people at the centre of its approach while recognising that lasting change requires collaboration across generations and sectors. We seek to connect youth energy and innovation with the knowledge and experience of farmers, communities, practitioners and partners.

Vision

Our Vision

A resilient and food-secure Africa where young people lead the transformation towards sustainable food systems, restored landscapes and climate-resilient communities.

Mission

Our Mission

To equip and mobilise young people to work with farmers and communities in advancing sustainable agriculture, agroforestry, food security and locally driven climate solutions.

How We Work

Our Approach

Youth-Led Action

We believe young people should have meaningful opportunities to learn, lead, innovate and contribute to the transformation of Africa's food systems and communities.

Community-Centred Solutions

We value solutions developed with communities rather than for them, recognising local knowledge, priorities and lived experience.

Practical Innovation

We encourage the responsible use of technology, data and practical agricultural innovations to address real challenges faced by farmers and communities.

Collaboration

We believe resilient food systems and communities require partnerships across sectors, disciplines and generations. We seek to learn from and collaborate with farmers, young people, public institutions, researchers, civil society and responsible private-sector actors.
