Partner With RAFO

Let's Build Resilience Together

RAFO welcomes conversations with individuals, communities, organisations and institutions interested in building more resilient food systems and communities.

Whether you are interested in collaboration, knowledge sharing, supporting an initiative or learning more about our work, we would be glad to hear from you.

How to Reach Us

Ways to Connect

Partner & Collaborate

RAFO is interested in collaborations that contribute to sustainable agriculture, resilient food systems, agroforestry, youth participation and locally driven climate action.

Knowledge & Learning

We welcome opportunities to exchange knowledge, learn from practitioners and communities, and contribute to conversations around agriculture, food security and climate resilience.

Support Our Work

Individuals and organisations interested in supporting RAFO's developing initiatives are welcome to start a conversation with us.

Youth & Community Engagement

We welcome connections with young people, farmers and communities interested in contributing to practical approaches to resilient food systems and climate action.
