Our Focus Areas

RAFO's work is organised around four focus areas and cross-cutting priorities that connect sustainable agriculture, food systems, youth participation and climate resilience.

Climate Change & Policy Advocacy

Supporting communities to adapt to changing climatic conditions through practical, locally relevant approaches to climate resilience.

Learn More

Biodiversity Conservation & Research

Promoting approaches that strengthen ecosystems, biodiversity and landscape restoration alongside productive agriculture.

Learn More

Indigenous Community Development

Working with communities to strengthen local knowledge, participation and leadership in sustainable food systems.

Learn More

Food Security & Water Access

Advancing food security through sustainable agriculture, improved access to nutritious food and stronger community resilience.

Learn More

Sustainable Agriculture Technologies

Exploring practical technologies and approaches that connect innovation with real challenges faced by farmers and communities.

Learn More

Capacity Building & Leadership

Creating pathways for young people to develop skills, contribute to their communities and participate in sustainable agri-food systems.
