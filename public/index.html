Who We Are

Young People Building Resilient Food Systems and Communities

Resilience for Africa's Future (RAFO) is a youth-led and youth-focused organisation based in Accra, Ghana, working to advance sustainable agri-food systems, agroforestry, sustainable agriculture and climate action.

RAFO believes Africa's young people have a critical role to play in building resilient food systems and communities. By equipping and mobilising young people to work alongside farmers and communities, RAFO seeks to promote practical solutions that strengthen food security, create opportunities, restore landscapes and build resilience to climate change.

Our work begins in Ghana, with a vision to contribute to a more resilient and sustainable future across Africa.

Our Vision

A Resilient and Food-Secure Africa

A resilient and food-secure Africa where young people lead the transformation towards sustainable food systems, restored landscapes and climate-resilient communities.

Our Mission

Equip, Mobilise and Work Together

To equip and mobilise young people to work with farmers and communities in advancing sustainable agriculture, agroforestry, food security and locally driven climate solutions.

What Guides Our Work

Our Focus Areas

Four connected areas shape how RAFO is developing practical, youth-led responses to food-system and climate challenges.

Sustainable Agriculture & Food Systems

Supporting practical approaches to sustainable food production, food security and resilient agri-food systems.

Agroforestry & Landscape Restoration

Exploring ways to integrate trees, productive agriculture, environmental stewardship and landscape restoration.

Youth Agripreneurship, Innovation & Leadership

Creating pathways for young people to build skills, apply innovation and participate meaningfully across agri-food systems.

Climate Action & Community Resilience

Working with communities around locally relevant responses that strengthen resilience to climate change.

Food Security

Food Security Starts Close to Home

RAFO believes building resilient food systems also means recognising the productive potential around us. From farms to household spaces, practical food-growing solutions can contribute to household nutrition, resilience and a stronger connection to food.

This is an emerging direction in RAFO's work. We are interested in approaches that help households make productive use of suitable available space while strengthening awareness of food production and environmental responsibility.
