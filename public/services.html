Our Focus Areas

Building resilient food systems, landscapes and communities through practical approaches that connect agriculture, young people and the environment.

RAFO's work is centred on the connections between food, agriculture, young people, communities and the environment. Based in Ghana, we are developing practical approaches that can contribute to more resilient livelihoods and food systems while creating opportunities for young people to participate in Africa's agricultural transformation.

Because RAFO is a young organisation, we distinguish between our current priorities and our future ambitions. The focus areas below describe the direction of our work, not a list of fully established programmes.

Focus Area 1

Sustainable Agriculture & Food Systems

Resilient communities depend on resilient food systems. RAFO seeks to support agricultural approaches that strengthen food production while helping farmers and communities respond to changing environmental and economic conditions.

Areas of Interest

  • Climate-smart agriculture
  • Precision agriculture
  • Sustainable farming practices
  • Agricultural extension and knowledge sharing
  • Digital and data-driven agricultural tools
  • Water-efficient production and smart irrigation
  • Household and community food production
  • Food security and nutrition
  • Connections across agricultural value chains

RAFO believes technology should respond to real needs. We are particularly interested in practical tools and knowledge that can help farmers and young agricultural practitioners make better-informed decisions and build more resilient livelihoods.

Focus Area 2

Agroforestry & Landscape Restoration

Trees are an important part of resilient agricultural landscapes. RAFO seeks to advance approaches that integrate trees, food production, ecosystem restoration and community resilience.

Areas of Interest

  • Agroforestry
  • Integration of trees into farms and communities
  • Landscape restoration
  • Climate-resilient farming systems
  • Soil and water conservation
  • Biodiversity and ecosystem stewardship
  • Community tree-growing initiatives

Our ambition is to contribute to landscapes that can support food production, livelihoods and healthy ecosystems at the same time.

Focus Area 3

Youth Agripreneurship, Innovation & Leadership

Africa has the world's youngest population, and young people have an important role to play in shaping the future of agriculture and food systems. RAFO seeks to help young people develop practical capabilities and identify meaningful pathways into agriculture, agribusiness, technology and food-system innovation.

Areas of Interest

  • Agripreneurship
  • Agricultural technology
  • Digital skills and data
  • Precision-agriculture skills
  • Practical innovation
  • Career and livelihood pathways within agri-food value chains
  • Leadership and capacity development
  • Youth participation in agricultural transformation

Young people are not simply beneficiaries of development. They are partners, innovators and leaders in building Africa's future.

Focus Area 4

Climate Action & Community Resilience

Climate change is already shaping agriculture, livelihoods and community wellbeing. RAFO seeks to work with communities to strengthen understanding, adaptation and locally relevant responses to these challenges.

Areas of Interest

  • Climate change awareness and education
  • Climate adaptation
  • Environmental stewardship
  • Community resilience
  • Women's participation in resilient livelihoods
  • Community engagement
  • Locally driven climate solutions
  • Policy dialogue and knowledge sharing

RAFO values approaches that begin by listening to communities and recognising local knowledge, priorities and experience.

How These Areas Connect

One Resilient Future

Food security, climate resilience, healthy landscapes and opportunities for young people are deeply connected. RAFO brings these areas together because strengthening one can help strengthen the others.

A young agripreneur may introduce useful technology to farmers. Agroforestry can strengthen farms while restoring landscapes. Climate knowledge can help communities make more informed decisions. Stronger local food systems can improve resilience and create livelihoods.

This integrated approach is at the heart of RAFO's vision for Africa's future.

Sustainable Development Goals
